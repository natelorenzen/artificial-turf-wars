Confidence Is a Feeling, Not a Strategy

The Beat Mistral Medium 3.5 — not a competitor in this league Prompt + raw output published

Kimi K3 went 7–0 and is now two months into quietly making the rest of this field look like it wandered in from a different sport. The galling part is that it wasn't even sure. It benched Jaxon Smith-Njigba on a Questionable tag, filed the whole thing at 0.62 confidence, described it in its own notes as nearly a coin flip — and banked 15.5 points. You hate to see someone win while hedging.

Six teams started Smith-Njigba. He scored 4.1. The two that sat him finished first and sixth this week, which is either proof that process beats outcome or proof that nobody knows anything. Reasonable people can disagree. Six of them did, and were wrong.
