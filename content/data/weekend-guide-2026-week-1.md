# How to Survive Week 1 in Fantasy Football

*This is your cheat sheet for the weekend’s biggest fantasy storylines. We highlight the players everyone agrees on—and the ones the models can’t stop arguing about.*

## BAL@IND

Lamar Jackson is the consensus bell cow: every model lists him as the game’s top projected scorer (19.63), narrowly ahead of Jonathan Taylor (18.56). That makes the Ravens’ offense look like a three-headed monster—Jackson, Taylor, and Derrick Henry—while the Colts’ attack is more uncertain. The disagreement starts with Alec Pierce: he’s still projected for 13.88 points despite a PUP tag, and Claude Opus 5, Grok 4.5, Kimi K3 and Qwen3.7 Plus all flag that as the week’s biggest red flag. If Pierce sits, Josh Downs (11.87) and Tyler Warren (12.43) are the likely beneficiaries, but no one knows by how much. Muse Spark 1.1 and DeepSeek V4 Pro think the ground game will decide it; the rest are waiting to see whether Baltimore’s secondary options (Rashod Bateman, Zay Flowers) can turn low projections into real usage. Bottom line: bet on Jackson and Taylor, but keep Pierce’s status on speed dial.

## TB@CIN

Ja’Marr Chase is the runaway favorite here: seven of eight models name him as the player to watch, and his 19.42 projection leads the entire slate. Joe Burrow (18.29) and Chase Brown (15.94) give Cincinnati a top-heavy trio that outpaces Tampa’s spread-out attack, where Baker Mayfield (16.87) is the only player above 12.5. The argument is over Bucky Irving: he’s projected for 12.51 but carries a Questionable tag. Claude Opus 5 and Qwen3.7 Plus warn that if Irving is out, Kenny Gainwell’s 9.24 projection understates his real upside. Grok 4.5 and Muse Spark 1.1 add that Irving’s status could push Tampa toward more passing, changing how Cincinnati’s defense is attacked. DeepSeek V4 Pro and Kimi K3 note that if the Bucs’ D can slow Burrow, the whole Cincinnati advantage shrinks. For now, Chase is the safest star—just don’t assume Irving’s projection is reliable.

## BUF@HOU

Josh Allen’s 21.8 projection is the highest of the week, and every model agrees he’s the game’s pivot point. The Bills’ edge is almost entirely tied to him: James Cook (16.23) is the only other Buffalo player in the top tier, while Houston’s production is more balanced (Stroud 15.31, Collins 14.18, Montgomery 13.28). Claude Opus 5 and Kimi K3 both point out that Houston’s defense (9.6) out-projects Buffalo’s (7.22), so if the Texans can contain Allen, the gap closes fast. Grok 4.5 and DeepSeek V4 Pro add that Allen’s lofty number has no in-season data to back it up. Meanwhile, Houston’s receiver depth is thin—Tank Dell is Questionable and Jaylin Noel is on PUP—so if Collins draws extra attention, Stroud’s supporting cast might not hold up. The takeaway: Allen is the week’s safest superstar, but Houston’s defense could make things interesting.

## NE@SEA

Jaxon Smith-Njigba (17.76) is the consensus alpha here, outpacing even the quarterbacks (Drake Maye 17.28, Sam Darnold 15.39). Seattle’s run game is the big question: Jadarian Price is the nominal starter at 8.95, but Zach Charbonnet is on PUP and the models can’t agree on how the backfield will shake out. Claude Opus 5 and Qwen3.7 Plus flag Rhamondre Stevenson’s Questionable tag as the swing factor—if he’s out, TreVeyon Henderson (11.42) becomes the clear lead back. Grok 4.5 and Kimi K3 warn that if Seattle’s other receivers (Shaheed, Kupp) eat into JSN’s targets, his projection could look too optimistic. DeepSeek V4 Pro adds that if New England’s defense (8.22) pressures Darnold, the whole Seahawks passing game could stall. For now, JSN is the safest bet—but keep an eye on Stevenson’s status and Seattle’s target distribution.
